FOR KOREANS ABROAD AND EVERYONE CURIOUS ABOUT KOREAKFOOD Journal makes Korean cooking understandable without flattening the culture behind it.
Everyone remembers a table that felt like home.

KFOOD Journal began with a simple wish: to preserve the tastes we miss and make them possible in kitchens far from Korea.

For members of the Korean diaspora, a familiar dish can reconnect language, family, and memory. For readers new to Korean food, the same dish can open a door into a culture built around sharing.

We use the Korean names of dishes, explain unfamiliar ingredients clearly, and pair practical recipes with the stories that give them meaning.
