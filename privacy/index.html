TRANSPARENCY ABOUT DATA AND ADVERTISINGThis page explains how KFOOD Journal and the services we use handle information when you visit the site.

LAST UPDATED · JULY 21, 2026

KFOOD Journal provides recipes, food guides, and cultural stories. We collect only the information needed to operate, understand, and support the site.

Information processed when you visit

Our hosting and security providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, timestamps, referring page, and approximate location derived from an IP address. This information helps deliver the site, prevent abuse, and diagnose errors.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand aggregated site use, such as page views, traffic sources, and general device information. Depending on your region and consent choices, analytics storage may be limited or disabled.

Google AdSense and cookies

KFOOD Journal uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers, and similar technologies to deliver, measure, limit, and protect ads. Ads may be personalized only where permitted and where required consent has been obtained; otherwise non-personalized or limited ads may be shown.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can review ad personalization choices in Google My Ad Center.

Consent and your choices

Where required, visitors are shown a Google-certified consent message before advertising cookies or similar technologies are used. You can accept, reject, or manage available choices in that message. Browser settings can also delete or block cookies, although some site features may work differently.

Information you choose to send

If you contact us or subscribe to the newsletter, we process the name, email address, subject, message, language, and page address you provide in order to respond or manage the subscription. Submissions are sent through a Google Form and stored in a private Google Sheet owned by KFOOD Journal. Google acts as a service provider for this processing. We do not sell personal information submitted through the site.

Retention, sharing, and security

Service providers process information only to provide hosting, analytics, advertising, security, or communication functions. Their own policies and retention periods may apply. We use reasonable safeguards and keep information only as long as necessary for these purposes or legal obligations.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. To ask a privacy question or make a request, use the contact page. We may need to verify a request before completing it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or applicable requirements change. The date above shows the latest revision.
