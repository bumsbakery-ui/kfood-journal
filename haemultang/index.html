Haemultang: The Korean Seafood Stew That’ll Transport You Straight to the Ocean

2025-06-06 · KFOOD JOURNAL

Hey there, seafood lovers and ocean dreamers! 🌊🦐 Missing the taste of the sea? Ready to dive into a bowl so packed with oceanic goodness that you’ll practically taste the salt air? Get ready to have your mind completely blown because I’m about to introduce you to Haemultang (해물탕) – Korea’s ultimate seafood extravaganza that’s basically the entire ocean partying in a spicy, bubbling hot pot!

Picture this: fresh crabs, plump shrimp, tender squid, juicy clams, and whatever other sea treasures you can get your hands on, all swimming together in a fiery red broth that’s been infused with Korean chili magic. The whole thing is steaming hot, incredibly fresh, and so packed with seafood that you’ll need a strategy just to eat it all! This isn’t just stew – it’s a full-scale oceanic celebration! 🦀🔥

Why Haemultang Will Ruin You for All Other Seafood Dishes Forever

Okay, real talk! My first haemultang experience was at a Korean seafood restaurant right by Busan’s coast, and I thought I was just ordering “some kind of seafood soup.” When that massive pot arrived – and I mean MASSIVE, like feed-a-small-village massive – I was completely unprepared for what I was about to experience.

The server lit the burner underneath, and as the stew started bubbling, the most incredible aroma hit me. It was like the ocean itself was calling my name! And when I took that first spoonful – fresh crab meat, spicy broth, tender vegetables – GAME OVER. I literally understood why people travel across the world for authentic Korean seafood!

The genius of haemultang is how it captures the pure essence of the sea while adding that signature Korean spicy kick. Every bite is different – sometimes you get sweet crab, sometimes briny clams, sometimes tender squid – but it all comes together in this harmonious seafood symphony that makes you feel like you’re dining right on the ocean! 🎵🌊

Everything You Need for This Oceanic Adventure

The Seafood All-Stars (The Main Event!)

  • 2 whole blue crabs, cleaned and quartered
  • 1 lb large shrimp, shells on
  • 1 lb squid, cleaned and cut into rings
  • 1 lb littleneck clams, scrubbed clean
  • 1/2 lb mussels, scrubbed and debearded
  • 8 oz firm white fish (cod or halibut), cut into chunks

The Flavor Foundation

  • 8 cups seafood or chicken stock
  • 3 tablespoons gochugaru (Korean red pepper flakes)
  • 2 tablespoons gochujang (Korean chili paste)
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce
  • 2 tablespoons rice wine or mirin
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil

The Supporting Cast

  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2-inch piece ginger, julienned
  • 4 green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 Korean radish, julienned (or regular radish)
  • 1 bunch Korean crown daisy (or spinach)
  • 2 jalapeño peppers, sliced (optional)

Shopping Ocean Expedition 🛒

Korean markets are your seafood paradise! Look for the freshest seafood possible – if they have a live tank, even better! H-Mart, 99 Ranch, or any Korean/Asian market will have the best selection and freshest options.

Can’t find blue crabs? Dungeness or any fresh crab works great! No Korean crown daisy? Spinach, watercress, or even napa cabbage can substitute. Missing some seafood? Mix and match with whatever’s freshest – the key is variety and quality!

Pro tip: Many Korean markets sell “haemultang mix” with pre-selected seafood – totally legitimate and super convenient!

Let’s Create Some Ocean Magic!

Step 1: Seafood Prep Mastery (Freshness is Everything!)

The secret to amazing haemultang is super fresh, properly prepared seafood:

  • Clean crabs and quarter them (or ask your fishmonger to do this)
  • Devein shrimp but leave shells on for flavor
  • Clean squid and cut into rings and tentacle pieces
  • Scrub clams and mussels thoroughly under cold water
  • Check that all clams and mussels are tightly closed (discard any that are open)
  • Cut fish into bite-sized chunks
  • Everything should smell like fresh ocean, not fishy!

Step 2: The Spicy Base Building

  • Heat sesame oil in a large pot or Korean earthenware pot (if you have one!)
  • Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger until fragrant (2-3 minutes)
  • Add gochugaru and gochujang, stirring for 30 seconds
  • Add soy sauce, fish sauce, and rice wine
  • This creates your spicy flavor base – the foundation of greatness!

Step 3: The Seafood Symphony Begins

  • Add stock and bring to a boil
  • Add the hardest-to-cook seafood first: crabs and thick fish pieces
  • Simmer for 3-4 minutes
  • Add clams and mussels (they need time to open)
  • Add shrimp after 2 minutes
  • Add squid in the last 1-2 minutes (don’t overcook or it gets rubbery!)
  • Timing is everything – each seafood has its perfect moment!

Step 4: The Fresh Finish

  • Add radish julienne and let it cook for 2 minutes
  • Add green onions and Korean crown daisy in the last minute
  • Add jalapeño slices if you want extra heat
  • Taste and adjust seasoning – it should be spicy, savory, and oceanic!
  • Don’t oversalt – the seafood provides natural brininess!

Step 5: The Grand Ocean Presentation

  • Serve immediately in the cooking pot (traditional Korean style!)
  • Provide seafood crackers, picks, and small bowls for shells
  • Serve with steamed rice and banchan
  • Make sure everyone has napkins – this gets messy in the best way!

The Art of Haemultang Domination (It’s a Skill!)

Here’s how to tackle this seafood feast like a pro:

The Crab Mission: Use the provided crackers to get every bit of sweet meat from those crab shells – it’s worth the work!

The Clam Strategy: Slurp those clams right from the shell, then use the empty shell as a spoon for the broth!

The Shrimp Technique: Peel with your fingers – the shells add flavor to the broth, so they did their job!

The Squid Appreciation: These should be tender, not chewy – if they’re rubbery, someone overcooked them!

The Broth Worship: Don’t forget to slurp that incredible spicy broth between seafood attacks!

The Social Experience: Haemultang is meant to be shared – it’s bonding over a bubbling pot of ocean goodness!

Why Your Body Will Thank You for This Ocean Feast

Haemultang isn’t just incredibly delicious – it’s practically a health spa in a bowl:

  • Protein powerhouse from all that fresh seafood
  • Omega-3 rich for heart and brain health
  • Low calorie, high satisfaction – seafood is naturally lean
  • Mineral rich – iodine, zinc, and selenium from the sea
  • Immune boosting from garlic, ginger, and spicy elements
  • Metabolism kick from all those spicy Korean chilies

Creative Ocean Adventures

Ready to make waves with your haemultang? Try these variations:

  • Luxury Ocean: Add lobster, scallops, or abalone for special occasions
  • Spice Level Management: Adjust gochugaru for your heat tolerance
  • Noodle Addition: Add glass noodles or udon for extra heartiness
  • Vegetable Power: Load up with mushrooms, zucchini, and bean sprouts
  • Coconut Fusion: Add a splash of coconut milk for creamy richness

The Coastal Culture Connection

Haemultang represents Korea’s deep connection to the sea, especially in coastal cities like Busan, Incheon, and Jeju Island. This dish emerged from fishing communities where fresh daily catches were transformed into communal feasts that brought families and neighbors together.

The tradition of cooking haemultang in earthenware pots over open flames creates not just a meal, but an experience. The bubbling pot becomes the centerpiece of the table, encouraging conversation, sharing, and the kind of relaxed dining that builds relationships.

In Korean culture, sharing haemultang is considered a gesture of abundance and hospitality. It’s often served during celebrations, family gatherings, and special occasions when you want to show guests the very best of what the ocean provides.
