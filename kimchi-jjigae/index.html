Kimchi Jjigae Recipe

2025-05-09 · KFOOD JOURNAL

Hello everyone! Kfood Journal here. Today I’m excited to share the perfect recipe for Kimchi Jjigae (Kimchi Stew), an essential dish on Korean dining tables.

The History of Kimchi Jjigae: A Stew Born from Fermentation Wisdom

Kimchi Jjigae is a quintessential Korean stew that harnesses the deep flavors of well-fermented kimchi. Since ancient times, Koreans possessed the wisdom to transform aging kimchi into a delicious, hearty stew rather than discarding it. The lactic acid bacteria and umami developed during kimchi’s fermentation process harmonize perfectly with pork and tofu, creating a stew that has become Korea’s ultimate “soul food” – warming both body and spirit, especially during cold winter days.

Ingredients (Serves 2)

Main Ingredients

  • 1 cup aged kimchi (about 200g)
  • 150g pork belly
  • 1/2 block tofu (about 150g)
  • 1/4 onion
  • 1 green onion
  • 2 shiitake mushrooms (optional)
  • 1 green chili pepper (for heat adjustment)
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon perilla oil

Seasonings

  • 1 tablespoon Korean red chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1/2 tablespoon soup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons kimchi juice (if available)
  • 1 teaspoon salted shrimp (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon sugar
  • Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Cooking Process

1. Prepare the Ingredients

  1. Cut the kimchi into 2-3cm pieces using scissors.
  2. Slice the pork belly into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Cut the tofu into 2cm cubes.
  4. Slice the onion and cut the green onion diagonally.
  5. Remove the stems from shiitake mushrooms and slice them.

2. Sauté the Kimchi

  1. Heat perilla oil in a pot over medium heat and sauté the pork first.
  2. When the pork is partially cooked, add the kimchi and continue to sauté for about 5 minutes.
  3. When the kimchi becomes translucent and well-coated with the pork fat, add the onion and sauté for another 2 minutes.

3. Create the Broth

  1. Once the kimchi and pork are well-sautéed, add 2 cups of water.
  2. When it starts to boil, add red chili powder, minced garlic, soup soy sauce, kimchi juice, salted shrimp (if using), and sugar. Stir well.
  3. Simmer on medium heat for about 10 minutes to allow the flavors to infuse into the broth.

4. Finishing Touches

  1. Add the tofu and shiitake mushrooms and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  2. Add the green onion and chili pepper, simmer for 1 minute more, then turn off the heat.
  3. Adjust the saltiness if needed (Note: Be cautious with salt as the kimchi and seasonings already contain salt).

5 Key Points for Perfect Kimchi Jjigae

  1. Well-Fermented Kimchi – Kimchi Jjigae tastes best when made with properly soured kimchi. Use kimchi that has been fermented for at least 2 weeks.
  2. Sauté the Kimchi Thoroughly – Sautéing the kimchi before adding water maximizes its umami flavor. Make sure to sauté until the kimchi becomes translucent.
  3. The Magic of Perilla Oil – Using perilla oil instead of sesame oil adds a deeper flavor dimension. Its unique nuttiness creates perfect harmony with kimchi jjigae.
  4. Simmering Time – The longer you simmer Kimchi Jjigae, the deeper the flavor becomes. Simmer for at least 15 minutes to allow the ingredients to fully infuse into the broth.
  5. The Power of Earthenware – If possible, cook and serve in an earthenware pot (ttukbaegi) or stone pot. It retains heat longer, allowing you to enjoy the stew piping hot until the last spoonful.

Variation Ideas: Kimchi Jjigae Reinvented

Tuna Kimchi Jjigae

Replace pork with 1 can of tuna. Don’t drain the oil – add it to the stew for a richer flavor profile.

Army Stew-Style Kimchi Jjigae

Enhance your basic kimchi jjigae by adding Spam, sausages, bacon, and rice cakes for a hearty army stew (budae jjigae) style variation.

Mushroom Kimchi Jjigae (Vegan Version)

Replace pork with an assortment of mushrooms (shiitake, enoki, oyster) and use vegetable stock made from kelp and dried shiitake mushrooms.

Serving Tips

  • Kimchi Jjigae is best enjoyed piping hot. Serve it bubbling at the table.
  • While delicious on its own, Kimchi Jjigae pairs perfectly with freshly steamed white rice.
  • Complete your meal by serving with 2-3 simple side dishes.
  • If desired, finish with a sprinkle of chopped green onions, sesame seeds, or a drop of sesame oil.

The Comfort of Kimchi Jjigae on Rainy Days

On a rainy day, surround yourself with the spicy aroma of bubbling Kimchi Jjigae while listening to raindrops tapping on your window. The moment you take your first spoonful, the warmth that spreads throughout your body provides comfort like no other. Kimchi Jjigae is more than just food – it’s a dish infused with Korean sentiment and memories, truly a “soul food.” Why not wash away today’s fatigue with a hearty bowl of homemade Kimchi Jjigae, shared with family or enjoyed in your own quiet moment?
